# HeaderHavoc privacy policy

Effective 22 November 2026

HeaderHavoc is a Chrome extension that sets or removes HTTP headers on URLs you configure.

## What HeaderHavoc collects

Nothing. HeaderHavoc has no account, no server and no analytics. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share
personal information or usage data.

## What it stores on your device

The profiles you create — URL patterns, header names and values, and which ones are switched on — are saved in
your browser using Chrome's local extension storage. A short record of the most recent rule update (when it
happened, how many rules were applied, and any errors) is saved the same way so the diagnostics panel can show it.

This data stays on your device. It leaves only if you use **Export** to save it to a file yourself.

## Where your header values go

HeaderHavoc makes no network requests of its own. The header values you enter are sent only as part of the HTTP
requests you configured them for, to the sites that match your URL patterns.

## Permissions

- **declarativeNetRequest** — applies your header rules.
- **declarativeNetRequestFeedback** — lets the diagnostics panel show which rules are active and which ones matched.
  This information stays on your device.
- **storage** — saves your profiles locally.
- **Access to all sites** — required because you choose which URLs the rules apply to. HeaderHavoc does not read
  the content of the pages you visit and does not inject scripts into them.

## Deleting your data

Uninstalling HeaderHavoc deletes everything it stored.
